# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers should make this community welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, focus on the work, respect privacy, and acknowledge mistakes. Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, doxxing, and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and spaces where someone officially represents the project. Report conduct concerns privately to edisonmacaraegmalasan@gmail.com. Include links and context while minimizing personal information. Reports will be handled with discretion; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required to address harm or comply with law.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, warn participants, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude participants for serious or repeated violations. Actions should consider impact, severity, and prior conduct. Maintainers are accountable for applying this policy fairly. Appeals may be sent to the same reporting address; this small project has no independent appeals body.
